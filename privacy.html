Sinilaa Privacy Policy

This app is used only by Sinilaa to schedule posts to our own TikTok and Threads accounts and to reply to comments on our own posts with product links.

We access our own account information, our posts, and the public replies people leave on our posts. Reply text and usernames are stored only to avoid answering the same reply twice and are deleted after 90 days.

We do not sell or share any data. To ask about or delete your data, email aliahnadhirah97@gmail.com.
